# Runbook: e-prescription and the portal notices

For whoever runs the platform. It covers what the worker does about prescriptions the provider did not confirm, what a prescription that is "in progress" for a long time means, and the notices the portal sends when the staff change something about a person.

## A prescription stays "in progress"

A prescription is valid only when the provider confirmed it. Until then it is `pending_submission`, and the worker (queue `erx.submit`, and the pass `erx.reconcile` every two minutes) keeps asking, with a wait that doubles from a minute to an hour.

| ------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `delivery_state = retrying`                | The provider was down or refused to answer; nothing reached it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Nothing: the next attempt sends it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| `delivery_state = awaiting_reconciliation` | The provider may hold the prescription and the answer was lost. The next attempt asks for the key BEFORE it sends anything.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Nothing: it adopts what the provider holds, or sends once.                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| `provider_in_flight_until` in the future   | A request was SENT and its outcome is not known (it timed out, the call threw, or the adapter said it cannot tell), or the process died after the mark. It may still be running there. For ten minutes (`inFlightPinMs`), counted from the moment of the send, nothing is sent again, cancelled or registered by hand, not even by a person; the screen says `prescription_provider_in_flight`. | Wait. If the provider is slower than ten minutes in the worst case, raise the window in `createPrescriptionsWiring` (it must be at least the processing time plus the socket timeout). A refusal the adapter claims came before the request left (`outcomeUnknown: false`) does not pin. |
| `attempt_lease_until` in the future        | A request holds the row without a connection, for at most two minutes. A crashed process leaves a lease that simply runs out.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Wait two minutes.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| `delivery_state = needs_attention`         | The matricula lost its verification or changed, or the provider holds a prescription that cannot be recorded for this one.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | The professional reads the reason on the screen. The operator verifies the matricula again if that is the cause.                                                                                                                                                                         |

A pass of `erx.reconcile` asks the provider with a timeout for every question, holds no row while it waits, and stops after three prescriptions in a row that the provider did not answer (or five minutes): the rest wait for the next pass, two minutes later. A line of the worker log says so ("the provider did not answer").

A prescription whose provider was disconnected can be closed by its professional with `acknowledgeUnknownOutcome`, to cancel it or to register it by hand (the trail says `unconfirmed`): it is closed here without asking, because there was nobody to ask. While a provider is connected the provider is always asked first. An owner or an administrator cannot close a prescription for them (they never read one): this is a known gap, recorded for the next review.

## Notices of the portal

Three notices warn a person that the staff changed something that concerns them. None carries a link, a code, a value or a name.

- **The record changed.** A change of the e-mail or the birth date of a patient's record is written by the database in the same transaction (`patient_record_change`, by a trigger: nobody else inserts). The sweep `portal.notice_sweep` (every minute, per organization) tells the address the record HAD and clears it from the table; the text says only that "a change was registered in data associated with this address", never a patient, a file or what changed. Each notice is claimed in a short transaction, sent with nothing open and marked in another one; a send that fails is logged ("a notice about a change to a record could not be sent") and tried again after 1, 5, 15 and 60 minutes, five times at most. A row without an address (the record had none) tells nobody. If the worker is stopped the notices wait in the table; after seven days, or after the fifth failure, the notice is GIVEN UP: the address is cleared, the entry `portal.notice.expired` is written, the log says "notices about a change to a record expired unsent", and the public registration of that patient closes (see below). A stream of `expired` entries means the mail provider is not delivering: look there first.
- **An account moved to another address** and **a representative was given access to a minor.** Queued in the transaction of the change (`portal.notice_send`). A failed delivery is retried by the queue; the entry `portal.notice.sent` of the trail is written when it is sent, so its absence is how a notice that did not go out shows.

**A record that was given an address from nowhere.** When a change gives an e-mail to a record that had none (nobody was told, because there was nobody to tell), or its notice was given up, the PUBLIC registration of that patient (document number plus e-mail) stays closed even after the seven days: whoever typed the address could otherwise register and read the portal. The patient is invited by an owner or an administrator (`POST /patients/:id/portal-invitation`), which writes `portal.registration.opened` and opens it again; reception is refused (`portal_owner_or_admin_required`). A patient whose address was corrected and who finds the public registration silent should be invited by an administrator after the organization checked who they are.

Both queues keep running when an organization is read-only (they protect a person). For seven days after a change of the e-mail or the birth date of a record, reception cannot invite the patient or a representative: an owner or an administrator does it (`portal_recent_identity_change`).
